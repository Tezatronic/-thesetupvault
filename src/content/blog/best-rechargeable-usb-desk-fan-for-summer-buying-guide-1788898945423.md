A fan that dies after an hour won’t survive a typical workday. Look for a capacity that delivers at least 6‑8 hours on a full charge. The KIMMOO’s 3000 mAh pack gives roughly 8.5 hours, which covers most full‑day sessions.

### 2. Noise Level
Background chatter and keyboard clicks already compete for attention. A fan that rattles above 45 dB can become a distraction. The lowest speed on this model stays around 35 dB, quiet enough to stay in the background.

### 3. Speed Control
Adjustability matters. A fan with a single low/high switch forces you to compromise. The KIMMOO offers 100 adjustable speed levels, letting you fine‑tune airflow from a gentle breeze to a more forceful gust.

A sturdy plastic housing and a stable base prevent wobbling when you turn up the speed. The KIMMOO feels solid enough to stay put on a wobble‑prone desk.
