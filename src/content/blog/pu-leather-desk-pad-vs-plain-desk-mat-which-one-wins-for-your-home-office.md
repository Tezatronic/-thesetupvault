## Who This Isn’t For

If you only need a temporary surface for occasional laptop use, a plain desk mat may be sufficient. Likewise, if you prefer a highly textured surface for a mechanical mouse that thrives on friction, the smoothness of PU leather could feel too slick. Finally, anyone who expects a fully recyclable product might look elsewhere, as PU leather isn’t biodegradable.
