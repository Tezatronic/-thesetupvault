## Key Factors to Evaluate

### 1. Load Capacity
A stand must support the weight of your laptop plus any accessories you might place on top (e.g., a mouse). Look for a capacity that comfortably exceeds the weight of your heaviest device. The Lamicall stand handles up to 11 lbs (5 kg), which covers everything from a 13‑inch ultrabook to a 15‑inch workstation‑class laptop.

### 2. Build Material
Aluminum alloy provides a balance of sturdiness and a sleek, modern look. It dissipates heat better than plastic and feels solid under load. The Lamicall’s aluminum construction also gives it a premium aesthetic that blends well with most desk setups.

### 3. Size Compatibility
Your stand should cradle the entire chassis without wobbling. Compatibility with laptops from 10 inches up to 17.3 inches ensures you can use the same stand if you upgrade or switch devices.

### 4. Portability vs. Stability
A heavier stand (around 2.3 lbs) is less likely to tip over, but it’s still light enough to move between rooms. If you travel frequently, a foldable or ultra‑light model might be preferable, but for a dedicated home office the added stability is worth the modest weight.

### 5. Cable Management
While not a hard spec, integrated channels or a low‑profile base can keep power and peripheral cables tidy. The Lamicall design leaves enough clearance for a power cord and a USB‑C hub without crowding the workspace.

### 6. Aesthetic Fit
Since a home office often doubles as a living space, the visual appeal of the stand matters. A brushed aluminum finish matches most modern desks and accessories.
