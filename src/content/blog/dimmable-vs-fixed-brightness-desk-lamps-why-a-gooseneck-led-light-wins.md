## Why Dimmable Beats Fixed‑Brightness

### Adapt to Different Tasks  
Reading, video calls, and detailed design work each demand a different light intensity. With a dimmable lamp you can dial the brightness down to a soft 1 % for late‑night reading or crank it up to 100 % for color‑critical work. Fixed‑brightness lamps leave you either squinting or dealing with glare.

### Reduce Eye Strain  
Modern LEDs can be tuned to a warmer 2700 K for relaxed evenings or a cooler 6500 K for focused daytime work. The ability to adjust both temperature and intensity means you can keep your eyes comfortable throughout the day.

### Save Energy (and Money)  
Turning the light down when you don’t need full power cuts electricity use. Even a 10 W LED will draw less when dimmed, extending the lifespan of the 100 LED beads that are rated for 60,000 hours.
