## What Makes a Paper Organizer Worth Buying?

### 1. Tray count and layout  
A five‑tier system gives you enough separation to sort by project, priority, or document type without taking up the footprint of a full‑size filing cabinet. Open‑front trays let you see the top edge of each stack, so you never have to pull the whole organizer out to find a sheet.

### 2. Material and durability  
Iron mesh construction provides a balance of strength and breathability. The open weave prevents paper from sticking together in humid rooms and lets dust fall through, extending the life of both the organizer and the documents inside.

### 3. Size relative to your desk  
You’ll want a footprint that sits neatly beside your monitor or keyboard. The dimensions of a good organizer are roughly a foot wide and a foot deep, leaving room for a mouse pad, a lamp, or a coffee mug.

### 4. Portability  
A built‑in handle is a small but handy feature. It lets you slide the organizer from a home office to a coworking space, or tuck it into a closet when you need extra floor space for a project.

### 5. Aesthetic fit  
A mesh finish blends well with modern minimalistic setups, especially when paired with a sleek metal monitor stand or a wooden desk pad. It’s also neutral enough to match a variety of color schemes.
