**Short answer:** The Lamicall Adjustable Laptop Stand can become the heart of a clean, efficient workstation by adding a few inexpensive accessories, routing power and data cables smartly, and using its adjustable height and angle to create a stable platform for everything from a USB‑C hub to a wireless charger.

Below is a step‑by‑step guide that shows exactly how to transform this aluminum‑alloy stand into a **multipurpose laptop stand charging hub** while keeping your desk organized.

## 1. Why Use a Laptop Stand as a Hub?

A laptop stand is already a raised, sturdy platform. When you treat that platform as a mini‑dock, you gain three immediate benefits:

| Benefit | How it helps |
|---------|--------------|
| **Reduced clutter** | All peripherals sit on one surface instead of scattering across the desk. |
| **Better ergonomics** | The stand’s height (5.7‑8.1 in) and angle (45‑180°) let you position the screen at eye level while keeping accessories within easy reach. |
| **Simplified power flow** | A single power strip or USB‑C hub can sit on the stand, feeding the laptop and any attached devices from one outlet. |

Because the Lamicall model supports laptops up to 11 lb (5 kg) and a size range of 10‑17.3 in, it can comfortably hold most work‑or‑play machines plus a modest accessory stack.

## 2. Choose the Right Accessories

### a. USB‑C Hub with Power Delivery

A hub that offers **PD charging** (e.g., 60 W) lets you power the laptop while using extra ports for a mouse, external SSD, or Ethernet. Place the hub directly on the stand’s flat top; the 360‑degree base rotation means you can spin the hub to keep cables on the side that best fits your desk layout.

> For a deeper dive on selecting a hub, see our guide on [USB‑C hubs with PD charging](/blog/what-to-know-about-usb-c-hubs-with-pd-charging-for-desk-setups/).

### b. Wireless Charging Pad

If your phone or earbuds support Qi charging, a slim wireless pad fits neatly beside the hub. Because the stand’s aluminum surface is non‑conductive to the pad, you won’t interfere with charging.

### c. Small Desk Lamp

A gooseneck or clip‑on LED lamp can be perched on the stand’s edge, keeping the light source close to the screen without taking up desk real‑estate. Check out our [USB desk lamp setups for small desks](/blog/usb-desk-lamp-setups-for-small-desks-how-a-gooseneck-clamp-lamp-saves-space/) for ideas.

### d. Cable Organizer Clips

Mini‑clips or Velcro straps attach to the stand’s underside or the side of the hub, corralling power cords, HDMI, and USB cables. This keeps the “spaghetti” from spilling onto the floor.

## 3. Set Up the Cable Management System

1. **Route power first.** Plug the stand’s power strip into the wall, then run the strip’s cord through a desk grommet or under‑desk channel.  
2. **Create a “cable island.”** Gather all laptop‑related cords (power, HDMI, USB) and bundle them with a reusable tie. Position the bundle so it rests on the stand’s base, not hanging off the edge.  
3. **Label each line.** Use color‑coded tags (see our [step‑by‑step cable labeling guide](/blog/step-by-step-cable-labeling-color-coding-with-nettbe-reusable-ties/)) so you can quickly identify which cable powers the hub, which feeds the monitor, etc.  
4. **Secure the hub.** Many hubs have rubber feet; add a non‑slip mat if you plan to rotate the stand frequently.  

The result is a tidy “cable island” that sits on the stand, keeping the rest of the desk surface free for writing, sketching, or placing a coffee mug.

## 4. Optimize Height and Angle for Different Use Cases

Because the Lamicall stand adjusts from **5.7 in to 8.1 in** in height, you can fine‑tune the laptop’s screen to match an external monitor or to sit at eye level for video calls. The angle range (45‑180°) lets you:

| Scenario | Recommended angle |
|----------|-------------------|
| Typing with a keyboard on the desk | 90° (flat) |
| Watching a tutorial while typing | 120°‑130° |
| Using a stylus or drawing tablet on top | 150°‑180° (almost flat) |

When the stand is set to its highest position, the extra clearance underneath is perfect for sliding a thin **wireless keyboard** and a **mouse** without crowding the desk. If you need a more compact setup, lower the stand and keep the hub at a steeper angle.

## 5. Turn the Stand Into a Charging Hub

Here’s a quick wiring diagram you can replicate:

1. **Wall outlet → Power strip** (mounted under the desk).  
2. **Power strip → USB‑C hub** (plugged into the hub’s PD port).  
3. **Hub → Laptop** (via USB‑C).  
4. **Hub → Phone wireless charger** (placed on the side).  
5. **Hub → External SSD / Ethernet** (via dedicated ports).  

All devices draw power from the same source, which reduces the number of cords you need to manage. Because the stand itself weighs only about **2.3 lb**, the added accessories won’t compromise stability as long as the total load stays under the **11 lb** capacity.

## 6. Keep the Desk Organized Around the Hub

- **Use a desk mat** that’s slightly larger than the stand. It protects the surface and gives a defined “zone” for the hub and accessories.  
- **Place frequently used items** (pen holder, sticky notes) on the mat but outside the stand’s footprint. This visual separation reinforces the hub’s purpose as a tech zone.  
- **Store spare peripherals** (USB drives, adapters) in a small drawer or a magnetic strip mounted on the desk’s side panel.  

By confining all electronic gear to the stand, you free up the rest of the workspace for creative tasks.

## 7. When to Upgrade the Setup

If you find yourself needing more than the stand can hold—say, a second monitor or a heavy external GPU—you might consider a dedicated monitor arm or a larger desk‑mounted charging station. The Lamicall stand excels as a **multipurpose laptop stand**, but it isn’t a full‑size docking station.

## 8. Potential Drawbacks

- **Weight limit:** The 11 lb capacity is generous for most laptops, but it won’t support a heavy workstation‑grade laptop paired with a large external monitor on top of the stand.  
- **Surface area:** The flat top is sizable enough for a hub and a few accessories, yet it can feel cramped if you try to stack too many items. Keeping the accessory count to a handful maintains stability.

Now that you’ve turned the Lamicall Adjustable Laptop Stand into a clean, functional hub, you may want to map out where every piece of furniture and gear will live before you buy more accessories. A simple layout plan can save you from future clutter and help you spot unused space.

## 10. Recap

- Use the stand’s adjustable height (5.7‑8.1 in) and angle (45‑180°) to position the laptop ergonomically.  
- Add a USB‑C hub with PD, a wireless charger, a compact lamp, and cable clips to create a **multipurpose laptop stand charging hub**.  
- Route all power and data through a single “cable island” on the stand, labeling each line for quick troubleshooting.  
- Keep the total load under the 11 lb capacity and avoid over‑crowding the top surface.  

By treating the Lamicall Adjustable Laptop Stand as a central platform rather than just a riser, you gain a tidier desk, fewer stray cords, and a more pleasant workflow—all without buying a full‑size docking station. Happy organizing!
