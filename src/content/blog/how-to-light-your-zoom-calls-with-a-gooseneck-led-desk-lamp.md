### Why a Dedicated Light Beats Your Laptop Screen

Your laptop’s built‑in display is designed for viewing, not for lighting. It often creates a harsh, uneven wash that leaves your face in shadow and makes you look washed‑out. A purpose‑built lamp gives you control over three critical variables:

| Variable | What the lamp lets you control | Why it matters for video |
|----------|------------------------------|--------------------------|
| **Color temperature** | 2700 K – 6500 K, stepless | Match daylight or warm indoor light to keep skin tones natural. |
| **Brightness** | 1 % – 100 % dimming | Reduce glare while still being bright enough for the camera. |
| **Direction** | Flexible 17‑inch light bar on a gooseneck | Position the light exactly where you need it without moving your monitor. |

Because the lamp runs on a 10 W USB adapter (included), you can power it from any USB‑C hub or wall charger—no extra outlet required.

### Setting Up the Airlonv Lamp for Video Calls

1. **Clamp it securely** – The metal clamp fits desktops up to 2.36 inches thick, so it will hold on most standard desks and even on a sturdy monitor stand.  
2. **Extend the gooseneck** – Bend the neck until the light bar sits just above eye level, angled slightly downward (about 15°). This mimics natural overhead lighting and avoids the “top‑lighting” look that creates deep shadows under the chin.  
3. **Choose the right color temperature** – For most indoor calls, 4000 K to 5000 K gives a neutral daylight feel. If you’re in a room with warm décor, dial it down to 3500 K to blend with the surroundings.  
4. **Set the brightness** – Start at around 30 % and increase until your face is evenly lit without blowing out the background. The stepless dimming means you can fine‑tune it in 1 % increments.

### Positioning the Lamp: Key Light vs. Fill Light

A single lamp can serve as either a **key light** (the main source) or a **fill light** (softens shadows from another source). Here’s how to decide:

| Goal | Setup |
|------|-------|
| **Key light only** | Place the lamp directly in front of you, about 12‑18 inches away, angled down 15°. This creates a clean, professional look with minimal equipment. |
| **Key + fill** | Keep the lamp as the key light, then add a second, softer light (or a window) on the opposite side at a lower intensity. The lamp’s 17‑inch bar spreads light evenly, reducing the need for a separate fill lamp. |

If you already have a window behind you, the lamp becomes the key light and the natural light acts as a fill. If the room is dark, consider a cheap LED panel as a secondary fill source.

### Fine‑Tuning Color Temperature and Brightness

- **Warm rooms (2700 K‑3500 K)** – Use the lower end of the lamp’s range to complement existing lighting and avoid a cold, clinical look.  
- **Bright office spaces (5000 K‑6500 K)** – Raise the temperature to match daylight, which helps the camera’s auto‑exposure stay consistent.  
- **Low‑light environments** – Increase brightness gradually; the lamp’s 10 W output is strong enough to illuminate a typical home‑office desk without overheating.
