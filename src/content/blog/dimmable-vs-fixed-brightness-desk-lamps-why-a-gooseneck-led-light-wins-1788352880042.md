## Why Dimmable Lamps Matter

### 1. Task‑specific lighting
- **Reading or coding:** Cooler light (around 6500K) reduces eye strain and boosts focus.  
- **Video calls or design work:** Warmer light (around 2700K) creates a more natural skin tone and softer ambience.  
- **Late‑night sessions:** Dimming down to 1 % of the lamp’s output protects your circadian rhythm and prevents headaches.

A dimmable lamp lets you slide between these extremes without swapping bulbs or adding extra fixtures.

### 2. Energy efficiency
Because the Airlonv lamp uses a 10 W USB‑powered LED array, lowering the output directly reduces power draw. You get the same light quality at a fraction of the energy cost compared with a fixed‑brightness lamp that always runs at full power.

### 3. Space‑saving ergonomics
The gooseneck design lets you position the 17‑inch wide light bar exactly where you need it—over a keyboard, a sketchpad, or a stack of books—without moving the entire lamp. The metal clamp grips desktops up to 2.36 inches thick, so it stays put on most work surfaces.

## Fixed‑Brightness Lamps: When They Still Work

Fixed‑brightness lamps are simple: plug in, turn on, and you’re done. They can be a good fit if:

- You have a **single, dedicated workspace** where lighting needs never change.  
- You prefer a **no‑adjustment** solution and don’t want to fiddle with controls.  
- Your budget is extremely tight and you can’t justify the extra features.

However, the lack of adjustability often leads to compromises: you might end up squinting on a bright day or straining your eyes in a dim room.

## Real‑World Scenario: Switching Between Tasks

Imagine you start the morning drafting a report. You set the lamp to a cool 6500K at 80 % brightness, which sharpens contrast on the screen and keeps you alert.
