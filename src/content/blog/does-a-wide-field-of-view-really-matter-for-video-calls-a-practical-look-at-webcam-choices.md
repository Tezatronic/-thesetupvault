### Why Field of View (FOV) Matters

Most built‑in laptop cameras capture a narrow slice of the room, usually around 60 degrees or less. That tight framing forces you to sit directly in front of the lens, and any movement can push you out of view. A webcam with a **90‑degree field of view** gives you a few extra inches on each side, which translates into:

* **More context for collaborators** – they can see a glimpse of your notes, a second monitor, or a whiteboard without you having to reposition the camera.
* **Comfortable framing for group calls** – if you’re on a call with a partner or a pet, the extra width keeps both of you in the picture.
* **Reduced “camera‑shaking” perception** – small head movements stay within the frame, making you look steadier.

In short, a wider FOV reduces the need for constant adjustments and helps you present a more natural, relaxed image.

### Making the Most of a Wide‑Angle Webcam

Even with a 90‑degree lens, positioning matters:

1. **Center the camera** – place it at eye level and align it with the middle of your monitor. This prevents a “looking down” effect.
2. **Mind the background** – a clean, uncluttered backdrop looks professional. A simple bookshelf or a tasteful wall art piece works well.
3. **Use the privacy cover** – slide it over the lens when you’re not on a call. It’s a quick visual cue that you’re not being recorded.
4. **Adjust lighting** – front‑facing soft light reduces shadows and makes the 1080p image pop.

These small steps amplify the benefits of a wide FOV and ensure you appear confident and organized on every call.
