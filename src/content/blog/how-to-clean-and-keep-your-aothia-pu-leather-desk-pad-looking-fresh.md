## Daily Care: The 2‑Minute Wipe‑Down

1. **Gather the right tools** – a clean microfiber cloth, a spray bottle of room‑temperature water, and (optional) a few drops of gentle dish soap.  
2. **Lightly mist the cloth** – you don’t want the pad soaking; a barely damp surface is enough.  
3. **Wipe in one direction** – start at the back edge and move toward the front, following the natural grain of the leather. This prevents streaks and pushes any dust toward the edges where it can be brushed away.  
4. **Dry immediately** – use a second dry microfiber cloth to pat the surface dry. The waterproof coating means you won’t damage the pad, but lingering moisture can encourage curling at the edges.

Doing this every day removes dust, fingerprints, and light residue before they set in.

## Weekly Deep Clean: Removing Stubborn Grime

### What you’ll need
- Mild liquid soap (a few drops of dish soap works well)  
- Warm water  
- Soft‑bristled brush or old toothbrush  
- A clean, lint‑free cloth

### Steps
1. **Mix a solution** – combine a few drops of soap with warm water in a small bowl.  
2. **Test a hidden spot** – dab a corner with the solution and wait a minute. If the color stays consistent, you’re good to go.  
3. **Apply with a brush** – dip the brush lightly, then gently scrub any areas with visible grime (e.g., around keyboard edges or mouse tracks).  
4. **Wipe clean** – use a damp cloth to lift the soap residue, then dry with a lint‑free towel.  
5. **Buff the surface** – finish with a dry microfiber cloth to restore the smooth glide.

Avoid abrasive cleaners, alcohol, or bleach; they can strip the PU coating and make the surface feel sticky.

## Spot‑Treating Common Stains

| Stain | Quick Fix |
|-------|-----------|
| **Coffee or tea** | Blot with a damp cloth immediately; follow with the mild soap solution if a ring forms. |
| **Ink** | Dab a cotton swab in isopropyl alcohol (70%); gently rub the spot, then wipe with a damp cloth. |
| **Food grease** | Sprinkle a tiny amount of baking soda, let sit for a minute, then wipe with a damp cloth. |

Always work from the outer edge of the stain toward the center to prevent spreading.

## How to Flatten a Curled Desk Pad

Curling usually starts at the edges after months of temperature swings. Two reliable methods work for PU leather:

### 1. Warm‑Press Method
1. **Lay the pad flat** on a clean surface.  
2. **Cover with a thin towel** to protect the leather.  
3. **Use a hair dryer on low heat** – sweep the dryer across the curled edge for 20‑30 seconds, keeping the nozzle moving to avoid overheating.  
4. **Press with a flat, heavy book** (or a stack of magazines) for a few minutes while the material cools. The heat relaxes the PU, and the weight holds it flat.

### 2. Weight‑Only Method (No Heat)
1. **Place the pad on a flat board** (a large cutting board works).  
2. **Lay a clean sheet of parchment paper** over the pad to keep dust out.  
3. **Stack heavy objects** (e.g., a stack of binders) evenly across the entire surface.  
4. **Leave for several hours or overnight**. The consistent pressure gradually flattens the edges without any heat.

Both methods are safe for the waterproof coating; just avoid direct high heat that could melt the PU.

## Routine Maintenance Checklist

| Frequency | Task |
|-----------|------|
| **Daily** | 2‑minute microfiber wipe |
| **Weekly** | Deep clean with mild soap solution |
| **As needed** | Spot‑treat spills immediately |
| **Monthly** | Check for edge curl; apply warm‑press or weight method |
| **Every 6 months** | Rotate the pad 180° to even out wear |

Following this schedule keeps the surface smooth for mouse tracking, protects the backing from slipping, and extends the pad’s lifespan well beyond the typical year‑to‑year turnover.
