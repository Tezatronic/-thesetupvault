## Why a USB Desk Lamp Works So Well in Tiny Setups

### 1. No extra outlet needed  
Most small desks have just one or two wall sockets, and they’re already busy powering monitors, chargers, and a laptop. A lamp that runs off USB sidesteps the need for a separate plug, letting you plug it into a laptop, a power bank, or a USB hub.

### 2. Clamp frees up surface area  
The metal clamp on this lamp fits desktops up to 2.36 inches thick, so it can attach to a thin tabletop, a bookshelf edge, or even a monitor stand. Once clamped, the lamp’s 17‑inch wide light bar hovers over your work area without taking up any desk space.

### 3. Flexible gooseneck positioning  
The gooseneck joint lets you angle the light exactly where you need it—whether you’re typing, sketching, or video‑chatting. Because the lamp is lightweight, you can reposition it dozens of times a day without it sagging.

### 4. Adjustable color temperature and brightness  
A range from 2700 K (warm) to 6500 K (daylight) lets you match the lighting to the time of day or the task at hand. Stepless dimming from 1 % to 100 % means you can keep the glare low for late‑night work or crank it up for detailed drafting.

### 5. Long‑lasting LEDs  
With 100 LED beads rated for a 60,000‑hour lifespan, the lamp will outlive most laptops and monitors, so you won’t be swapping bulbs every few years.

## How to Install the Lamp on a Small Desk

1. **Position the clamp** – Slide the metal clamp onto the edge of your desk, making sure the surface is clean and flat. Tighten the screw until it feels secure but don’t overtighten.  
2. **Route the USB cable** – Plug the lamp into a USB‑C port on your laptop, a power bank, or the included adapter. Run the cable along the desk edge or through a cable‑management clip to keep it out of the way.  
3. **Adjust the gooseneck** – Bend the arm until the light shines directly onto your work area. The joint holds its shape, but you can readjust as needed.  
4. **Set color temperature and brightness** – Use the touch‑sensitive control on the base to cycle through warm to cool light and to dim or brighten.  

These steps take under two minutes, and once set up the lamp stays exactly where you left it.
