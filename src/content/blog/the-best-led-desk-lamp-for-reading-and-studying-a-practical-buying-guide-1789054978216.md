## Why Light Quality Matters When You Read or Study

Long‑hour reading sessions put a lot of strain on the eyes. A lamp that lets you fine‑tune brightness and color temperature can reduce fatigue, improve focus, and keep your posture upright (you won’t have to hunch over a dim surface). Here are the physiological reasons:

- **Blue‑rich light (higher Kelvin) boosts alertness**, which is useful for early‑morning study blocks.
- **Warmer light (lower Kelvin) relaxes the eyes**, ideal for late‑night reading.
- **Smooth dimming prevents flicker**, a common cause of headaches.

Choosing a lamp that lets you move seamlessly between these settings means you can match the lighting to the time of day and the type of material you’re working with—textbooks, PDFs, or code editors.

## Key Factors to Consider When Picking an LED Desk Lamp for Reading

1. **Adjustable Color Temperature**  
   A range from 2700 K (soft warm) to 6500 K (daylight) lets you customize the ambience. Look for stepless control so you can land on the exact hue that feels comfortable.

2. **Fine‑Grained Brightness Control**  
   Dimming from 1 % to 100 % gives you the ability to keep glare off the page while still having enough illumination for detailed work.

3. **Light Distribution**  
   A wide light bar (around 17 inches) spreads light evenly across a typical textbook or laptop screen, reducing hot spots.

4. **Mounting Flexibility**  
   A sturdy gooseneck with a metal clamp that fits desktops up to about 2.36 inches thick lets you position the lamp exactly where you need it, even on a crowded desk.

5. **Power Source**  
   USB power (5 V/3 A) means you can plug the lamp into a laptop, power bank, or wall adapter, keeping cable clutter low.

6. **LED Lifespan**  
   100 LED beads rated for 60,000 hours mean you won’t be swapping bulbs for years.

7. **Physical Footprint**  
   A compact base and a flexible arm keep the lamp out of the way when you’re not using it.

When you line up these criteria, you’ll quickly see why the Airlonv model checks almost every box for a study‑focused setup.
