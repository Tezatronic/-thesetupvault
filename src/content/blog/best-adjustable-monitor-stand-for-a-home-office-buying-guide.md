## Why an Adjustable Monitor Stand Matters
Spending hours in front of a screen can take a toll on your neck, shoulders, and lower back. Raising the monitor to eye level reduces the need to tilt your head, which in turn eases strain and improves posture. A stand also frees up desk real‑estate, letting you keep a keyboard, notebook, or a small plant within reach without crowding the work surface. In a home office where space is often at a premium, a good monitor riser can make the difference between a cramped workstation and a tidy, efficient setup.

## Key Factors to Evaluate

### Height Adjustability
Look for a stand that lets you raise or lower the screen in smooth increments. Some models use a simple screw mechanism, while others rely on gas‑spring arms. For most home‑office needs, a modest range that gets the top of the display to roughly eye level is sufficient.

### Build Quality and Material
A metal platform feels more stable than plastic, especially when you lean on the monitor or adjust it frequently. The WALI stand uses a sturdy metal shelf topped with anti‑skid rubber pads, which keep the screen from sliding and protect the desk surface.

### Weight Capacity
Make sure the stand can hold the weight of your monitor. The WALI model is rated for up to **44 lbs (20 kg)**, which covers the majority of mid‑size LCD and LED panels on the market today.

### Footprint
A compact footprint leaves more room for other accessories. The dimensions of the WALI stand are **14.6 × 9.3 × 5.5 inches (L × W × H)**, a size that fits comfortably on most desks without overwhelming the workspace.

### Stability and Anti‑Skid Features
Rubber pads on the bottom of the platform prevent wobble and protect your desk from scratches. This is especially important if you have a glass or polished surface.

### Compatibility
While the WALI stand does not include a VESA mount, its flat platform works with any monitor that can sit on a surface. If you need a mount that attaches directly to the back of the screen, you’ll have to look elsewhere.

### Aesthetics
A sleek, low‑profile design blends better with modern home‑office décor. The WALI stand’s simple black finish keeps the focus on your monitor rather than the stand itself.

After weighing these considerations, the WALI Computer Monitor Stand checks most of the boxes for a functional, budget‑friendly option.
