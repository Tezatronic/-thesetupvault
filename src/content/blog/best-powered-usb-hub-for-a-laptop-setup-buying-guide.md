## Why a Powered Hub Matters for Laptop Workstations

Most modern laptops ship with only a couple of USB‑C ports and maybe a single USB‑A. When you start adding a mouse, external SSD, webcam, and a few peripherals, you quickly run out of space. A **powered USB hub** solves two problems at once:

1. **More ports without draining the laptop’s battery.** The hub’s own 65 W adapter supplies power, so your laptop can stay plugged in or run off its battery while the hub powers the attached devices.  
2. **Consistent high‑speed performance.** With USB 3.2 support, you get up to 10 Gbps transfer speeds—crucial for large file transfers from an external SSD or for a 4K webcam feed.

If you’re building a dedicated desk for remote work, video editing, or gaming, a reliable hub is as essential as a good monitor stand or keyboard.

## Key Factors to Evaluate When Choosing a Powered USB Hub

When you start looking at options, keep these criteria in mind. They’ll help you separate the useful models from the ones that sound impressive but fall short in real‑world use.

### 1. Port Configuration

A laptop‑centric hub should give you a blend of USB‑C and USB‑A. The intpw hub offers **1 USB‑C 3.2 port, 2 USB‑A 3.2 ports, 4 USB‑A 3.0 ports, and 2 USB‑C PD 45 W charging ports**. That layout covers most peripherals: a USB‑C external drive, a couple of high‑speed USB‑A flash drives, a keyboard, a mouse, and a couple of phones or tablets for quick charging.

### 2. Data Transfer Speed

Look for USB 3.2 (or “10 Gbps”) on the ports you’ll use for data‑intensive devices. Slower USB‑2.0 ports are fine for a mouse or keyboard, but an SSD or a 4K webcam benefits from the full 10 Gbps bandwidth.

### 3. Power Delivery (PD) Capability

A hub that can charge a phone or tablet is handy, but be aware of the **PD ports’ limitation**: they’re designed for phones/tablets, not for powering a laptop. If you need to charge a laptop through the hub, you’ll need a separate solution.

### 4. Build Quality and Design

Aluminum unibody construction feels sturdy and helps dissipate heat. The **32‑degree angled ergonomic design** keeps the ports accessible without bending your wrist, which is a subtle but welcome ergonomic win.

### 5. Cable Length

A **3.3‑foot cable** gives enough reach to place the hub wherever it’s most convenient on your desk while keeping the power brick tidy.

### 6. Included Power Adapter

A dedicated **65 W (20 V/3.25 A) power adapter** means you won’t have to rely on a weak laptop charger to keep everything alive.
