Below is a practical guide to choosing an ergonomic wireless set, configuring it for maximum comfort, and integrating it into a well‑balanced desk layout.

## Why Ergonomics Matters in a Home Office

Long‑hour typing forces the hands, wrists, and forearms into static positions that can lead to repetitive‑strain injuries. A keyboard that forces you to reach for the numeric keypad or a mouse that requires excessive grip pressure adds unnecessary stress. Wireless devices also free up desk space, allowing you to position everything at the optimal height and angle without tangled cords.

## Configuring the RECCAZR Combo for Maximum Comfort

1. **Place the keyboard at elbow height**  
   Sit with your forearms parallel to the floor and your elbows at a 90‑degree angle. The keyboard should sit just below the elbows, allowing your wrists to stay straight. If your desk is low, consider a low‑profile keyboard tray or a thin wrist rest.

2. **Use a wrist rest**  
   Even though the RECCAZR keys are quiet, the low‑profile design can still cause the wrists to dip. A gel or memory‑foam wrist pad keeps the wrists neutral and reduces pressure on the carpal tunnel.

3. **Adjust the mouse DPI to your workflow**  
   - **800 DPI** – Ideal for detailed work like spreadsheet editing or graphic design where you need fine cursor control.  
   - **1200 DPI** – Works well for general office tasks, striking a balance between speed and precision.  
   - **1600 DPI** – Good for large monitors or multi‑screen setups where you need to traverse the screen quickly.  
   Switch DPI on the fly using the mouse’s built‑in button; you’ll notice less hand movement and less fatigue.

4. **Position the mouse at the same height as the keyboard**  
   Keep the mouse on the same plane as the keyboard to avoid raising or lowering the wrist. If you use a mouse pad with a wrist groove, pair it with the RECCAZR’s silent clicks for a truly quiet experience.

5. **Leverage the multimedia shortcut keys**  
   Map the most used functions (e.g., mute, volume up/down, play/pause) to the dedicated keys. This reduces the need to reach for the mouse or on‑screen controls, keeping your hands on the home row.

6. **Set the auto‑sleep timer**  
   In the keyboard’s driver software (if you install it), set the sleep timeout to a few minutes. The combo will go to sleep when you’re not typing, conserving battery and keeping the device cool.
