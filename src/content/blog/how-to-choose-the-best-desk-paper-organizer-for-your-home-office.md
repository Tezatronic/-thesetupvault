## Key Factors to Consider

### 1. Tray Count and Layout  
A five‑tier layout gives you enough separation to categorize documents (e.g., “To‑Do,” “In‑Progress,” “Reference,” “Bills,” and “Archive”). More trays can be useful, but they also increase the footprint, which may be a problem on a very small desk.

### 2. Material and Build Quality  
Iron mesh provides a sturdy feel while keeping the organizer lightweight. Look for a finish that resists rust if you live in a humid climate.

### 3. Dimensions  
The organizer should fit comfortably on your desk without encroaching on your primary work surface. The model we recommend measures **12.79 × 9.25 × 11.22 inches (L × W × H)**—a size that balances capacity with a modest footprint.

### 4. Portability  
A built‑in carry handle is a small but handy feature. If you often move between a home office and a shared workspace, the handle lets you transport the whole unit in one go.

### 5. Aesthetic Compatibility  
Mesh organizers tend to have an industrial look. Choose a finish that matches the other metal accents on your desk, or contrast it with a wooden or matte‑black setup for visual interest.

### 6. Accessibility  
Open‑front trays let you see the top of each stack at a glance. If you need privacy for sensitive documents, consider a separate lockable drawer or a cover.
