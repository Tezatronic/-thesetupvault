## Why Adjustability Matters

A static laptop on a flat desk forces you to hunch, which strains the cervical spine and can lead to chronic discomfort. An adjustable stand lets you:

* **Raise the screen** to match your eye line, reducing neck flexion.  
* **Tilt the keyboard** forward or backward, allowing a more natural wrist posture.  
* **Rotate the base** for left‑handed users or for quick cable management.  

These adjustments are especially valuable in a home office where you might share the desk with a monitor, a drawing tablet, or a small lamp.

### 2. Angle Flexibility
A good ergonomic stand should let you set the screen anywhere from a flat position to a near‑vertical stance. With an angle range of 45 degrees to 180 degrees, you can find the sweet spot for typing, reading, or video calls.

### 3. Load Capacity
If you own a heavier 17‑inch laptop or a laptop with a docking station, you need a sturdy platform. This stand supports up to 11 lbs, covering the majority of consumer laptops.

### 4. Build Material
Aluminum alloy provides a balance of strength and heat dissipation. It feels solid, resists wobble, and helps keep the laptop cool during long sessions.

### 5. Compatibility
A versatile stand should accommodate a wide range of laptop sizes. The Lamicall fits devices from 10 inches up to 17.3 inches, covering everything from compact ultrabooks to larger workstations.

### 6. Portability & Weight
If you move between a home office and a coffee shop, a lightweight design matters. At roughly 2.3 lbs, the stand is easy to lift yet stable enough for daily use.

### 7. Ease of Adjustment
Knob‑style or lever‑type height and angle controls should be smooth and not require excessive force. The Lamicall’s single‑handed adjustment mechanism lets you switch positions in seconds.

### 8. Cable Management
A 360‑degree rotating base helps route power and peripheral cables without tangling, a small but handy feature for tidy desks.
