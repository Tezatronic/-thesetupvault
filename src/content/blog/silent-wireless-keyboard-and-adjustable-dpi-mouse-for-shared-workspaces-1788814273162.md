### Why Silence Matters in Shared Workspaces

When a desk is used by multiple people throughout the day, every click and keystroke adds up. A noisy mechanical keyboard can interrupt a colleague on a call, while a clicky mouse can distract anyone trying to focus. Silent input devices keep the ambient noise level low, fostering a more professional and courteous environment.

Beyond noise, a wireless setup removes the clutter of cables that can quickly become a tripping hazard in a high‑traffic area. A single nano USB receiver that plugs into any available port means you can move the combo between laptops or desktops without rewiring.
