### Key Factors to Consider When Choosing a Quiet Portable Desk Fan

When you’re hunting for a **portable desk fan** that won’t disturb coworkers, keep these criteria in mind:

1. **Noise level** – Look for fans that advertise a dB rating at their lowest speed. Anything under 40 dB is generally safe for a shared environment.
2. **Adjustable speed range** – More speed steps give you finer control over airflow without having to crank the fan to its maximum.
3. **Battery life** – A rechargeable fan should last through a typical workday on a single charge, so you’re not tethered to a socket.
4. **Power source** – USB charging is ideal because most desks already have a laptop or a USB hub.
5. **Size and weight** – A compact, lightweight unit won’t clutter a small desk and can be easily repositioned.
6. **Build quality** – Sturdy construction prevents wobble and ensures the fan stays quiet even at higher speeds.

The KIMMOO fan scores well across all these points, making it a solid fit for most coworking setups.
