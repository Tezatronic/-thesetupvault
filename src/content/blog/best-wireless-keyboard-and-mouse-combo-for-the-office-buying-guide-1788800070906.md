## Key Factors to Evaluate

Before you click “add to cart,” run through this checklist to ensure the combo you choose aligns with your office habits.

### Connectivity

A single nano USB receiver that plugs into any available USB port is the simplest solution. Look for a combo that advertises a stable 2.4 GHz link and an effective range of at least 10 meters. This keeps you from dealing with frequent reconnections or signal drop‑outs.

### Keyboard Layout

For most office tasks, a full‑size keyboard with a numeric keypad is a must. It speeds up spreadsheet work and reduces the need to switch between the keyboard and a separate number pad. Bonus points go to models that include multimedia shortcut keys for quick access to volume, play/pause, and screen brightness controls.

### Mouse DPI Options

Adjustable DPI lets you fine‑tune cursor speed. A three‑level DPI switch (800, 1200, 1600) covers everything from precise pixel‑perfect work in design apps to fast navigation across large monitors.

### Noise Profile

Silent or quiet keystrokes and mouse clicks are essential in open‑plan offices or when you’re on frequent video calls. Look for “quiet” or “silent” branding in the product description.

### Power Management

Auto‑sleep mode extends battery life by putting the devices into a low‑power state when idle. This means fewer battery changes and less maintenance.
