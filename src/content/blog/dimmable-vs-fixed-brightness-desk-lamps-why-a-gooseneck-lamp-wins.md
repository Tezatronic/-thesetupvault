### Why Dimmable Beats Fixed‑Brightness

When you sit at a desk for hours, the lighting needs of a video call, a spreadsheet, and a late‑night reading session are rarely the same. Fixed‑brightness lamps force you to compromise: you either end up with glare on a screen or a dim workspace that strains your eyes. A dimmable lamp solves that by letting you:

1. **Match ambient light** – Raise the brightness on a sunny day, lower it when the room is already lit.
2. **Shift color temperature** – Cool 6500K light for focused work, warm 2700K for a relaxed evening.
3. **Save energy** – Running a lamp at 1% uses a fraction of the power of a lamp stuck at 100%.

The flexibility alone makes a dimmable lamp a more ergonomic choice, especially when paired with a flexible gooseneck that can be positioned exactly where you need it.
